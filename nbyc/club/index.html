The Club

The Club

New Brolin Yacht Club is a members' club on the water in New Brolin. Ocean-side sailing near the Olympic Sailing Pavilion.

Photo: Mikerussell · CC BY-SA 3.0
Dining room and barBar and grill Wednesday to Sunday, member dinners on race nights.
MoorageAbout 140 slips and a guest dock.
Dry sail and hoistDry storage for trailered keelboats and a dinghy park, with a member-run hoist.
Reciprocal privilegesMembers are welcome at the other Albanardia clubs and at hundreds of yacht clubs around the country.
