Junior sailing at NBYC

Juniors

From a first sail in an Optimist to high school and AYRC racing. Junior members sail every day of summer camp and train year-round.

Photo: Dennis G. Jarvis · CC BY-SA 2.0

Programs

Green FleetAges 7 to 11 in Optimists. First races on short courses, lots of games and swimming.
Opti Race TeamAges 9 to 15 racing the full AYRC circuit and regional Opti regattas.
High School TeamAges 14 to 18 in C420s and Club FJs, with team racing for high school sailors.
ILCA TeamSinglehanded racing in ILCA 4 and 6 rigs for teens moving up from Optis.
29er Skiff SquadTrapeze and gennaker racing for older juniors on the Olympic pathway.
Catamaran WeekTeens in Hobie 16s: speed, flying a hull and beach launches.

Junior boats

Club-owned boats for the junior program. Juniors with their own boats are welcome in every group.

20 boatsOptimist

The first racing boat for most junior sailors.

10 boatsC420

The doublehanded trainer for high school and youth racing.

10 boatsILCA

The Olympic singlehander, in 4, 6 and 7 rigs.

6 boats29er

The youth skiff, with a trapeze and a big gennaker.

4 boatsHobie 16

The classic beach catamaran, fast and wet for teens and coaches.

4 boatsHobie Wave

An easy, boomless beach catamaran.

Summer at NBYC

Camp days, practice and regatta weekends.

Summer camps

Weekly camps from June 21 to August 20, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Beginners welcome; every camper swims a short test on the first day.

Year-round training

Spring and fall practices on weekends, winter rules and video nights in the clubhouse. Coaches are US Sailing certified, and every practice has a safety boat.

ScholarshipsNo one misses out for money. NESF youth grants and the NBYC junior fund cover camp, charter boats and travel.
