Rivalry Week

Brady the Bruin Brings the Purple and Gold to Silverbrooks

Aldense travels from New Brolin for its annual game against UA Silverbrooks, and its mascot is coming along on ALBExpress.

University News staff

Aldense University's Bruins play the UA Silverbrooks Mets this Saturday in the oldest rivalry in the state. The game sells out every year, and Aldense says about 3,000 students and alumni will make the trip from New Brolin, most of them on the ALBExpress X4.

Brady the Bruin, the purple bear in gold trim who has led Aldense's teams since the 1950s, will ride the 10:05 a.m. train and greet fans at the SBX Airport station on the way in. The Aldense Student Athletic Association asks riders to wear purple and gold, and to leave the cowbells at home.

Kickoff is at 1 p.m. Fans without tickets can watch at the Center for Student Leadership & Connection on either campus.
