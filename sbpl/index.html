Who can get a card

Anyone who lives, works or studies in Thompson County, including Marisfield, Whilliker, Evansville and the northwest suburbs. Your SBPL card works at all 14 locations. The Thompson County Library is a separate system.

About SBPL

Silverbrooks Public Library opened in 1796, making it the third-oldest library system in the United States and one of the biggest. It serves metro Thompson County from the Central Library downtown and 13 neighborhood branches. Its local history room holds the records of the Swedish and French settlers who built the city.
