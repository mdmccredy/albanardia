Volunteer

Hamilton Island Sailing Club runs on volunteers. No sailing experience needed for most jobs, and volunteers sail free on open days.

Dock handHelp sailors launch and land, and keep the dock tidy.
Safety boat driverDrive a chase boat during classes. Training and a boating license course provided.
Assistant instructorHelp with camps and classes, a great first step toward US Sailing instructor certification.
Front deskWelcome visitors, check out boats and answer the phone.
Boat maintenanceWinter work nights fixing boats, sails and trailers.
EventsOpen houses, fun races and fundraisers.
