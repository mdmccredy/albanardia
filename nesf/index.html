Northeastern Sailing Foundation

More people sailing, all over Albanardia.

NESF is the state's sailing foundation. We run the Albanardia Youth Racing Circuit, fund junior programs, train race officers and coaches, and keep boats in the hands of kids who'd otherwise watch from the dock.

Programs

Everything NESF does is about getting more people on the water and keeping them there.

Albanardia Youth Racing CircuitEleven summer regattas around the state for Optimist, ILCA, C420, Club FJ, RS Feva and 29er sailors.Youth sailing grantsGrants to clubs and sailing centers for boats, coaches and scholarships. 2026 grants reached 19 programs.Race management trainingUS Sailing club race officer and judge seminars every winter, so every club can run a good regatta.Coach developmentLevel 1 and Level 2 instructor courses, plus a paid mentorship for first-year coaches.Safety at SeaA one-day offshore safety seminar for big-boat sailors, with hands-on liferaft and fire training.Boat loan poolOptimists, C420s and ILCAs on loan to new programs and to sailors who can't charter.

Youth sailing grants

Clubs and community sailing centers can apply each year for up to $15,000 toward boats, coaching and scholarships. Applications open January 4 and close February 26, 2027. Decisions by April 1.

Ask about a grant

Boat loan pool

24 Optimists, 16 C420s, 14 ILCAs and 10 Larssen Club Radials, loaned for a season to new junior programs and to AYRC sailors who can't charter. Boats go out in April and come home in October.
