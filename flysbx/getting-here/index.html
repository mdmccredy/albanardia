Getting here

Getting to and from SBX

Train, bus, regional rail, rideshare, rental cars or your own car. Everything meets at the SBX Transit Center.

SBTrain D Line

From Nygård through downtown, under Silverbrooks Harbor to East Harbor, then under Elliott Bay to the port and SBX. About 18 minutes from downtown, every 6 minutes at rush hour. One SBCard fare plus the $3 airport station surcharge.

SBMetro buses

R Line rapid bus, 81 Airport–Rutherford local through Marisfield, and the 110 SBX Flyer express from Elliot Square through the I-95 Harbor Tunnel, all day. At night the N110 follows the D Line.

ALBExpress X4 and NETA Regional Rail

The X4 runs Elliot Square, Marisfield, SBX, Rutherford and New Brolin every 20 minutes. NETA Regional Rail trains to Waterloo and Rutherford stop here too.

AirLink people mover

Free, every 4 minutes, 24 hours. Seven stations: Terminal D, Terminal C, Terminal B, Terminals A & X, Central Garages, the Economy Lot and the Rental Car Center.

Free terminal shuttle

Run by the airport, every 5 minutes, 24 hours. Leaves the Transit Center and circles counterclockwise to Concourse X (International), A, B, C and D.

Rideshare and taxis

Uber, Lyft and taxis pick up on the Ground Transportation level of each garage. Follow the green signs from baggage claim.

Rental cars

Every rental company is in the Rental Car Center, one stop on the AirLink: Alamo, Avis, Budget, Dollar, Enterprise, Hertz, National, Sixt and Thrifty.

Driving

Follow the airport signs to Exit 4 for Airport Pkwy, the only road onto the airport. It meets Terminal Drive, a one-way loop around the garages and terminals.

See it on the map

Every road, garage, station and gate is on the SBX airport map.
