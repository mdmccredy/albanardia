Parking

Park at SBX

Six places to park, from the garages next to the concourses to the Economy Lot on the AirLink.

Garage AShort-term$6 per half hour, $54 per day

Closest to Concourses A and X. Best for pickups and short trips.

Garage BHourly$5 per half hour, $48 per day

Across from Concourse B. Covered walkway on the departures level.

Garage CDaily$38 per day

Next to Concourse C and the Delta check-in hall.

Garage DDaily and long-term$32 per day, $160 per week

Beside Concourse D. Ten floors, EV chargers on floor 2.

Economy LotLong-term$19 per day, $95 per week

Off Thompson Blvd, with free AirLink trains to every terminal every 4 minutes.

Cell Phone LotWaitingFree, up to 60 minutes

On Airport Pkwy inbound. Wait here until your traveler is at the curb.

Steps from the terminals

Garages A to D connect to every concourse by covered walkways on the departures level.

Good to know

  • Pay with a card or your phone at the exit. Garages A to D take E-ZPass.
  • Live space counts show on the signs along Terminal Drive.
  • EV charging in Garages C and D and the Economy Lot, at the regular parking rate.
  • Accessible spaces on the first floor of every garage, near the elevators.
  • Motorcycles park free in the marked areas of Garage D.

Picking someone up?

Wait free in the Cell Phone Lot. Terminal Drive is one-way, counterclockwise, around the garages and terminals, so if you miss your door, you'll need to circle. Arrivals pickups are on the lower level; rideshare and taxis are on the Ground Transportation level of each garage.
