The Silverbrooks Sailing Foundation

Keeping Silverbrooks on the water since 1958.

We fund the boats, the coaches and the scholarships behind Silverbrooks Yacht Club and the Silverbrooks Community Sailing Center, so every kid in the city can learn to sail.

Our mission

Sailing belongs to the whole city.

The Foundation began in 1958, when a few Silverbrooks Yacht Club members passed a hat to buy six Optimists for the junior program. Today it is the nonprofit home of two clubs: one at the end of Hanson Bay Marina, one open to everyone on Foundland Street.

Two clubs, one foundation

Where your gift goes sailing

Silverbrooks Yacht Club

At the outer end of Hanson Bay Marina on the north shore. Junior racing, summer camps and the home of the Hanson Bay Showdown, the AYRC Championship.

Visit SBYC →

Silverbrooks Community Sailing Center

Public sailing for the whole city on Foundland Street, from first sails and adaptive programs to a racing team. Opened by the Foundation in 1994.

Junior Sailing Scholarships

Need-based awards that cover camp, team dues and regatta travel. No sailor at SBYC or SBCS is turned away for cost.

Boat & Equipment Fund

Community

Free Sail Saturdays

Every summer Saturday the Community Sailing Center opens its docks: free rides and first lessons for anyone in the city.
