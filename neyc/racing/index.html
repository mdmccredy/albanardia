Racing at NEYC

Racing

7 one-design fleets race at Northeastern Yacht Club. Notices of race and sailing instructions are posted 30 days before each regatta.

Fleets

Active boats in each fleet for 2027.

12 boatsJ/105

A 34-foot keelboat with an asymmetric kite, raced by five.

10 boatsJ/80

A 26-foot sportboat for four.

8 boatsMC30

McCredy Yachts' 30-foot keelboat, like a J/105.

12 boatsJ/70

The world's most popular sportboat, raced by four.

6 boatsMelges 24

A 24-foot sportboat for five with a big asymmetric kite.

14 boatsILCA

The Olympic singlehander, in 4, 6 and 7 rigs.

8 boatsRS Aero

A light, modern singlehander in 5, 6, 7 and 9 rigs.
