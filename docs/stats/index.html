Big picture

Albanardia has about 3.54 million people on 6,550 sq mi, so it would rank 31st of the 50 states, a hair behind Utah (3.54M) and ahead of Nevada (3.28M), and it's more than three times the size of Delaware, which it replaces.

Land area ~6,550 sq mi A bit bigger than Connecticut, about 3x Delaware
Density ~525 per sq mi Between Connecticut (~760) and Delaware (~545)
Counties 13 Delaware has 3

Silverbrooks and its metro

Silverbrooks packs about 1,000,000 people into 76 sq mi of land, roughly 13,200 per sq mi. That's Austin's population at nearly Boston's density, and denser than Philadelphia.

Sailing: 22 yacht clubs and sailing centers statewide. Silverbrooks has 6 (SBYC, SBCS, Pearl's End, Everett YC, Notrhabite SC, Sjagette YC), Marisfield has Elliott Bay YC, Whilliker has Corinthian YC, and the public Killalea Sailing Center sits on Hanson Bay. The rural counties have Northmark YC, Stepford YC, Archila Community Sailing Center and Marianoe YC. Also college sailing at SBU and AlbTech, the Olympic Sailing Pavilion in New Brolin, and Larssen Sailcraft in Foxford.
