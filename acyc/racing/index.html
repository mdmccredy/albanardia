Racing at ACYC

Racing

13 one-design fleets race at Albanardia City Yacht Club. Notices of race and sailing instructions are posted 30 days before each regatta.

Photo: Lars Wehrmann · CC BY-SA 4.0

Active boats in each fleet for 2027.

30 boatsJ/70

The world's most popular sportboat, raced by four.

16 boatsJ/105

A 30-foot three-person keelboat with a deep fleet of serious racers.

10 boatsMelges 24

A 24-foot sportboat for five with a big asymmetric kite.

A 26-foot sportboat for four.

18 boatsMC23

McCredy Yachts' sportboat, like a J/70.

10 boatsMC30

McCredy Yachts' 30-foot keelboat, like a J/105.

6 boatsMC35

McCredy Yachts' flagship big boat for full crews.

6 boatsSanta Cruz 27

The classic ultralight downwind sled for four or five.

6 boats505

A 16-foot trapeze dinghy, one of the fastest two-person boats around.

12 boatsSnipe

Serious sailing, serious fun: a 15-foot doublehander raced around the world.

28 boatsILCA

The Olympic singlehander, in 4, 6 and 7 rigs.

16 boatsRS Aero

A light, modern singlehander in 5, 6, 7 and 9 rigs.
