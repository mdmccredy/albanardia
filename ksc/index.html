Killalea Sailing Club

A keelboat sailing club and school on Dock K in Hanson Bay Marina: learn to sail, then sail the club's J/22s, J/24s, J/80s and MC30s whenever you like. No boat to buy, no slip to rent and no bottom to paint.

Kids and dinghies?

Killalea is keelboats only and for adults. Youth camps, dinghy classes and junior racing are at SBYC Community Sailing, at the outer end of the same marina.
