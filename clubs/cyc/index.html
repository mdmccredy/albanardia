Corinthian Yacht Club of Silverbrooks

A racing club on the north shore of the bay, home of a strong junior team. An AYRC member club.

Become a memberRacing calendar

Programs

Racing, juniors and the social side of the club.

Junior racing teamYear-round coaching for the AYRC and high school regattas in ILCAs, C420s and Optis.
Adult racingWednesday night series, weekend regattas and a winter frostbite fleet.
Cruising and socialRaft-ups, the commodore's ball and the summer cruise to Hamilton Island.
