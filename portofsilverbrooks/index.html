Port of Silverbrooks

Moving Albanardia by air and by sea.

We run Silverbrooks Thompson International Airport and McKay Field, and we own the East Harbor docks and the Samolette Container Terminal, one of the biggest container ports on the East Coast.

Aviation

The Silverbrooks Airport Authority, part of the Port, runs both of the city's airports.

Silverbrooks Thompson International (SBX)

About 70 million passengers a year, four runways, five concourses and a Delta hub, on made land in the bay.

flysbx.com →
Silverbrooks McKay Field (MCK)

The city's first airport, opened in the 1930s in Nordfield. General aviation, government flights and some regional service, with a 7,000-foot main runway, corporate hangars and T-hangars.

1 Airport Way, Nordfield

Maritime

Two cargo ports on two harbors.

Samolette Container Terminal

On Samolette Harbor beside SBX. A deep-water wharf, 21 ship-to-shore cranes, a huge container yard, an on-dock rail yard and a truck gate off Port Rd. Opened in the 2010s.

The Port's original cargo docks in East Harbor, the older of the two cargo ports, on the downtown side of the harbor.

About the Port

The Port of Silverbrooks is a city agency that runs like a private company, with its own board. It built SBX on fill in the bay from 1968 to 1974, along with the SBTrain D Line under the harbor, and opened the Samolette Container Terminal in the 2010s. It runs on airport and cargo revenue, not property taxes.
